# Reports, sanctions and appeals

This page is for everyone who uses OpenTalk. If you moderate or run a server, see the [moderation guide for servers](../server-administration/moderation.md).

## What you see in the channel list

After a person's name, OpenTalk says what is special about them:

| Text | Meaning |
|---|---|
| owner, administrator, moderator | Their role on this server. Registered users have no label. |
| guest | Joined without an account. |
| muted, voice muted, text muted | A moderator stopped them talking, sending messages, or both, for a while. |
| restricted | They can join channels but cannot talk or send messages. |

## Reporting someone

If someone is abusive, select them in the channel tree or the Users list and choose **Moderation → Report selected user** (or press Shift+F10 and choose Report). Say what happened and press Enter. The server's moderators see your report and your name; the person you reported is not told who reported them.

## When a moderator acts on you

You are always told what happened, who did it and why, in the history and by your screen reader. For example: "Mute until 2026-11-02 14:30, from mod. Reason: shouting."

| Sanction | What it means |
|---|---|
| Warning | Nothing changes; it is a note on your record. |
| Mute | You cannot talk, send messages, or both, until the time shown (or until it is lifted). |
| Kick | You were disconnected. You can connect again straight away. |
| Ban | You were disconnected and cannot log in (or join that channel) until the time shown. The message gives the server's contact if it has one. |
| Restriction | You can join channels but cannot talk or send messages. |

OpenTalk does not reconnect by itself after a kick or ban.

## Appealing

If you have an account, you can appeal each sanction once:

1. **Moderation → My sanctions and appeals.**
2. Select the sanction in "Sanctions on you" and press **Appeal the selected sanction**.
3. Explain why it should be reconsidered and press OK.

Someone other than the moderator who gave the sanction decides, when anyone else can. You are told the decision (upheld, reduced or overturned) and the reason. The "Your appeals" list in the same dialog shows each appeal and its outcome.

Guests cannot appeal. If you were banned as a guest, use the contact in the ban message.

## Keyboard

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| Alt+O | Moderation menu |
| Shift+F10 or the Menu key | Actions for the selected person or channel, including Report |
| Ctrl+Shift+A | Administration (only for moderators and administrators) |
