# Code of conduct

OpenTalk is built by and for a diverse community, including many people who use screen readers and other assistive technology. We want participating in this project to be welcoming for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Make your contributions accessible. For example, describe images and screenshots in text.
- Respect people's privacy and their different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or demeaning comments, including about disability.
- Sharing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the project maintainers, through a private security advisory or by contacting a maintainer directly. Maintainers will review every report, keep reporters' identities confidential, and may take actions ranging from a warning to a ban from project spaces. Decisions can be appealed to a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision.
